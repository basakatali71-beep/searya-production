Project Transfer Checklist

Essential due-diligence steps before buying a project

1. Ownership and identity

  • Verify the seller's identity and authority to sell
  • Confirm ownership of the repository, domain, brand and third-party accounts
  • Review open-source licenses and contractor agreements

2. Technical review

  • Review the source code and commit history
  • Test the setup documentation in an independent environment
  • Rotate secret keys; do not accept the seller's old credentials
  • Verify hosting, database, storage and backups

3. Commercial metrics

  • Verify revenue in the payment provider's live dashboard
  • Review churn, refunds, traffic and operating costs
  • Confirm that customer data can be transferred lawfully

4. Agreement and payment

  • List every asset included in the transfer agreement
  • Define acceptance tests and delivery dates
  • Use an independent escrow provider when possible
  • Do not release full payment before acceptance is complete
