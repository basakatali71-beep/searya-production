Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

This policy explains what Searya collects, why it is used and the controls available to account holders.

Information we collect

We may process your name, email address, role and securely hashed password when you create an account; project descriptions and images in listings; conversation content in messages; and IP address and transaction timestamps for security. If you consent to analytics, we also measure an anonymous visitor identifier, page path, device category, referring domain, UTM campaign information, session duration and interface actions such as searches, filters, listing views, registrations, listing submissions or new conversations. Optional exit feedback may record a selected reason. Analytics never records passwords, message contents or form-field contents.

How we use information

  • Operate accounts, listings, messages and plan entitlements
  • Prevent fraudulent listings, abuse and security incidents
  • Send notifications and password reset emails
  • Measure product performance and improve the service

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal data. Searya does not receive card details; payment information is processed by the payment provider. Hosting, email, payment and error-monitoring providers may access only the data required to deliver their services. First-party behavior events are retained for up to 180 days and visitor sessions for up to 90 days; other retention periods are limited according to purpose, legal obligations and security needs.

Your rights

You can export or permanently delete your account data from My Account. You may also request access or correction through the reporting and support controls available in your account.

Security

Passwords are never stored in plain text. Session cookies use HttpOnly and SameSite protections. Even so, no transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
