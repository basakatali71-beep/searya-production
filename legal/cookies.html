Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Analytics are enabled only with your explicit consent and can be disabled from the preference panel.

Essential cookies

An HttpOnly session cookie is used to maintain a secure login. This cookie is required for account and security features.

Local preferences

Theme and certain interface preferences may be stored locally in your browser. They are not used for third-party advertising tracking.

Analytics and campaign measurement

When you consent, Searya stores an anonymous visitor identifier, viewed path, device category, session duration, referring domain, UTM campaign parameters and selected interface actions on its own server. These actions may include searches, filters, listing views, authentication status, listing submissions, conversation starts and optional exit-reason choices. Passwords, private messages and form-field contents are not collected for analytics. We currently use no third-party advertising pixel. Choosing “Essential only” deletes analytics records associated with that browser identifier.
