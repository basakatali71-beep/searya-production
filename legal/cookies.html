Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Analytics are enabled only with your explicit consent and can be disabled from the preference panel.

Essential cookies

An HttpOnly session cookie is used to maintain a secure login. This cookie is required for account and security features.

Local preferences

Theme and certain interface preferences may be stored locally in your browser. They are not used for third-party advertising tracking.

Analytics and campaign measurement

When you consent, Searya stores an anonymous visitor identifier, viewed path, referring domain and UTM campaign parameters on its own server. Conversions such as registration, publishing a listing and starting a conversation are associated with that anonymous identifier to measure campaign performance. We currently use no third-party advertising pixel. Choosing “Essential only” deletes these visit and conversion records.
