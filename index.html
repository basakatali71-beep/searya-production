Job Cost Calculator

Combine direct costs, overhead and markup into a defensible customer price.

Markup is applied to total cost. Gross margin is shown separately.

JOB COST SUMMARY

Build every cost into your quote

Job costing combines materials, labor and additional direct expenses. Overhead allocates operating costs such as insurance, software and vehicles. Markup then creates profit above the complete cost.

Markup is not margin

A 25% markup on $1,000 creates a $1,250 price and a 20% gross margin. Turn the result into a customer document with the estimate generator.

Why billable percentage matters

Client work is only part of a freelancer's week. Sales, administration, revisions and professional development reduce billable capacity, so dividing the annual target by every working hour usually underprices the work.

Add a pricing buffer

The 10% and 20% buffers provide room for scope changes and unpaid time. Compare the result with the profit margin calculator.

Understand your contribution margin

Each sale contributes its price minus variable cost toward fixed costs. Break-even units equal fixed costs divided by that contribution. Because partial units usually cannot be sold, the operational target rounds up.

Improve break-even performance

Raise contribution by improving price or reducing unit cost, then review the effect with the profit margin calculator.
