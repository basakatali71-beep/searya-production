Important safety warning

Searya only connects buyers and sellers.

Searya is not a party to any transaction, does not hold purchase funds and cannot guarantee a user's identity, ownership claims, source code, revenue or project quality. Before paying, verify the seller and the project yourself—preferably in person or through a live video call—and inspect the working product, source-code repository, ownership evidence and every account or asset included in the transfer.

Do not send payment until you fully trust the seller, understand exactly what you will receive and have agreed to the transfer in writing. Use an independent escrow service whenever possible.

Users are responsible for their own due diligence and transactions to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law. Nothing here limits rights or liabilities that cannot legally be excluded. Read the Terms of Use and Handover Checklist before completing a deal.
